CURRENT ASSEMBLY AND FIRMWARE NOTES
1. Approved simplified architecture: main devices receive individual external power connections. No Pi, LILYGO, LCD or Chameleon main power travels through this carrier. Use one power source per device; never parallel USB 5 V outputs.
2. JP2 peripheral supply: 1 +5V_EXT, 2 GND, 3 3V3_AUX, 4 GND. A regulated USB 5 V source feeds CardKB and the VIN of an external preassembled Pololu D24V10F3 #2830. Its regulated 3.3 V output feeds JP2.3. Do not swap either voltage or connect a bare battery cell.
3. ESP3V3 remains separate from 3V3_AUX. Grounds are common. Both LILYGO VSYS contacts and all 40 Pi header contacts remain NC. The Pi powers its retained HAT through its USB host connection; do not independently backfeed HAT power.
4. JP3 is an eight-pin harness to the preassembled Adafruit #757 I2C level shifter: 1 GND, 2 ESP3V3/LV, 3 +5V_EXT/HV, 4 SDA_LV/A1, 5 KB_SDA/B1, 6 SCL_LV/A2, 7 KB_SCL/B2, 8 GND. A/B are matched channels, not carrier component reference A1. Unused module channels remain unconnected. This is not the module physical footprint.
5. JP8 encoder: 1 GND, 2 ESP3V3, 3 SDA_LV, 4 SCL_LV. The assembled Adafruit5880 encoder uses I2C0x36; CardKB uses0x5F. Start with100kHz and check the assembled cable and parallel pullups.
6. R1-R4 are axial through-hole10k resistors. C1/C2/C5/C7 are through-hole100nF ceramic; C3/C4/C6/C8 are through-hole10uF electrolytic. Electrolytic pad1 is positive, pad2 GND. Q1/Q2 and R5-R8 are replaced by the assembled757; there are no loose SMD parts to hand-solder on the carrier.
7. Radio SPI: GPIO12SCK,11MOSI,13MISO; radio1GPIO10CSN/14CE; radio2GPIO15CSN/16CE. CardKB/encoderGPIO1SDA/41SCL. IR GPIO39RX/40TX. GPIO2,43,44 remain NC. No AUX_OE_N or translator-enable firmware remains.
8. Direct SPI/IR wires and the BSS138 keyboard converter are not power-off isolation. Use only with ESP and peripheral supplies present; disconnect signal harnesses before independently powering off/programming one domain. Configure radioCSN HIGH, CE LOW and IR_TX LOW before transmitting. Avoid unpowered devices being driven through GPIO protection paths.
9. The inline USB on/off switch controls only the external peripheral supply. It is not a deck-wide switch or Linux shutdown control. Coordinate ESP/peripheral power as above; shut down the Pi before removing its own power.
10. The external regulator nominal1A rating is not a verified load allowance. Measure the actual two PA/LNA radios, pulsed IR, rail droop and regulator temperature. The Miady bank total rating is shared by its ports; individual cables do not increase one bank total capacity.
11. All carrier electrical contacts are plated through holes. Assembled modules contain factory-soldered SMD components, with only through-hole headers/wires required for user assembly. Insulate and strain-relieve external supply wiring; verify polarity with a meter.
12. This audit verifies assignments and schematic connectivity, not physical fit, operating current, temperature or fabrication readiness. Native copper/DRC and actual hardware measurements are separate.
