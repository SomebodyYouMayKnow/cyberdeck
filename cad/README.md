# CAD sources

These files are copied from the active design folder for publication:

- `Cyberdeck_Enclosure.scad` is the editable parametric enclosure and printed-support concept for the 226 × 126 mm PCB with eight outside lips.
- `Cyberdeck_Fusion_Layout.dxf` is the updated millimetre placement sketch, including the PCB lips, 12 case holes, and eight 2.751 mm CardKB/Chameleon support holes.
- `Cyberdeck_layout.svg` previews the board position and current module reserves.
- `MECHANICAL_VALIDATION.md` records dimensions, assumptions and physical checks still required.

`NEONRED_PCB_component-mounts_2026-10-19.step` is the Fusion-exported STEP solid for the 1.6 mm carrier PCB. `Export_Neonred_PCB_STEP.py` regenerates it in Fusion 360. [`NEONRED_Case_Lid_Hinge_HDMI_2026-11-18.step`](NEONRED_Case_Lid_Hinge_HDMI_2026-11-18.step) is the refreshed Fusion STEP export of the case, lid and hinge assembly with the HDMI display opening and mounting features. The lid has a centered 76.6 × 51.8 mm screen opening, four 2.6 mm mounting holes, integral screw bosses and four removable corner clips. The screen envelope follows the existing 85 × 56.5 × 8.3 mm CAD reserve and the generic 3.5-inch HDMI display mentioned in README; the repository BOM does not identify a manufacturer/model. The source script is [`FusionScripts/AddHDMIScreenMount/AddHDMIScreenMount.py`](FusionScripts/AddHDMIScreenMount/AddHDMIScreenMount.py), and its geometry audit is [`fusion_screen_mount_audit.json`](fusion_screen_mount_audit.json). The STEP was exported on 2026-11-18. The enclosure is a prototype and has not been physically fit-tested; check the actual display revision, connector/cable orientation, keyboard, lid swing and print tolerances before final printing. PrusaSlicer exports mesh formats such as STL/3MF, not STEP. The CardKB and Chameleon support holes pass through the live EasyEDA PCB and align nominally to service-tray bosses. The latest Gerber package is [`PCB/NEONRED_Gerbers_current_2026-11-18.zip`](../PCB/NEONRED_Gerbers_current_2026-11-18.zip); older archives are in [`PCB/past gerbers/`](../PCB/past%20gerbers/). The ALFA cradle is separate and unplaced, with no board holes, because its location and exact adapter envelope are not established.
